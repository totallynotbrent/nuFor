---
title: 2D state, grid, and equation of state
---

The first step of the 2D extension adds the geometry and thermodynamics the 2D
Euler solver will march on; the fluxes and reconstruction are a separate step.

## Grid

`grid2d(nx, ny, xmin, xmax, ymin, ymax)` builds a uniform structured Cartesian
grid over `[xmin,xmax] x [ymin,ymax]` with `nx` by `ny` cells. Cell centers sit
at `(xmin + (i+1/2)dx, ymin + (j+1/2)dy)` in row-major order (`j*nx + i`), and
the `nx+1` vertical and `ny+1` horizontal faces close the domain exactly. Axes
are aligned, so a vertical face is a plane of constant x with unit normal
`(±1, 0)` and length `dy`, and a horizontal face has normal `(0, ±1)` and length
`dx` — the normals and lengths a flux step needs are available directly from
`dx`, `dy`, and the two face arrays.

## State

`ConservedState2d` holds the four conservative variables — density, x- and
y-momentum, and total energy — stored flat in row-major order. `prim_to_cons2d`
and `cons_to_prim2d` convert with `m_x = rho u`, `m_y = rho v`, `E = rho e_t`,
and `u = m_x/rho`, `v = m_y/rho`, `e_t = E/rho`, rejecting non-positive or
non-finite density.

## Equation of state

The ideal-gas relations generalise to two velocity components:

- pressure `p = (gamma - 1) rho (e_t - (u^2 + v^2)/2)`
- sound speed `a = sqrt(gamma p / rho)`
- Mach number `M = |v| / a`.

Each rejects non-admissible inputs. `check_physical2d` scans a state for
non-finite or non-positive density so a future solver stops cleanly.

Together these are the minimal 2D state/geometry/thermodynamics layer; the HLLC
flux, MUSCL reconstruction, and TVD limiter build on top of them next.
